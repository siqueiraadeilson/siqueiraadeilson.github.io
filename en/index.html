04 / FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Before
we talk.

The essentials about who I am, how I work, and how to get in touch.

Who is Adeilson Siqueira?

Adeilson Siqueira is a full stack developer and product engineer based in São Paulo, Brazil. He is the founder of Squood and the creator of Luable, Gamium, Ponteiro Studio, the game Steal Mutant Pet, and the Minecraft mods Auto Best Enchantments and Librarian Books Preview.

What technologies do you use?

React, TypeScript, JavaScript, and Node.js every day, with Vite and Zustand. Electron for desktop apps, Gun.js and WebRTC for real-time communication, Java with Fabric for Minecraft mods, and Batch for Windows automation.

Are you available for freelance work?

Yes. I am available for new projects, work remotely from Brazil, and reply within 48 hours by email, WhatsApp, or Fiverr.

What kind of projects do you build?

Digital products from start to finish: web platforms, desktop apps, tools for developers and creators, Minecraft mods, and Roblox games.

Where can I see your code?

Public repositories are on GitHub and the mods are on Modrinth. Each project also has its own page in this portfolio.
