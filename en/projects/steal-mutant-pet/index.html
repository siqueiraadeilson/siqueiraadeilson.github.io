Steal Mutant Pet: a multiplayer mutant pet tycoon on Roblox

A multiplayer Roblox tycoon: buy and steal eggs, discover mutations, collect mutant pets, and build your empire — stealing from other players along the way. Available for PC, console, mobile, and tablet.

Highlights

  • Steal eggs and pets from other players in servers of up to 6 players

  • Active beta, with evolution guided by player feedback

How the game works

You start by buying eggs and finding out what hatches. Each mutation brings rarer pets, and those pets build your empire. The other players on the server want the same thing, so stealing and defending are part of the game.

The core loop

  1. Buy eggs or steal them from other players
  2. Discover mutations and collect rare pets
  3. Grow your empire while protecting what you have earned

Active beta

The game is in beta and evolves with player feedback.
