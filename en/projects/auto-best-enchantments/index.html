How to get the right enchantment without rerolling villagers by hand

Getting a cheap Mending book can take hours of breaking and replacing a lectern. Auto Best Enchantments automates that loop: set what you are looking for, look at the lectern, and the mod repeats the process until the librarian villager offers the trade you want.

How to use it

  1. Add goals with /autolec add, setting the enchantment, level, and price
  2. Look directly at the lectern and run /autolec start
  3. Stop at any time with /autolec stop and review goals with /autolec list

Command examples

/autolec add minecraft:mending max min
Mending at max level for the lowest price
/autolec add minecraft:sharpness 5 5 any
Sharpness V at any price
/autolec add minecraft:unbreaking any 10 32
Any level of Unbreaking between 10 and 32 emeralds

Recommended setup

  • Axe in your main hand and several lecterns in your offhand
  • Villager locked in place, with the lectern easy to target
  • /autolec autoTrade to buy automatically when the trade appears
  • /autolec preserveTool to stop before your tool breaks
